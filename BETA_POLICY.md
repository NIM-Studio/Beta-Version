# NIM Studio Beta Release Policy

**Software version:** 0.1.0-beta  
**Policy version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 16 June 2026  
**Maintainer:** Sara Monteiro

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how the NIM Studio controlled beta programme operates. It is a public summary and does not replace the NIM Studio Proprietary Beta Software Licence or any separately signed beta-testing agreement. If documents conflict, the signed agreement and applicable licence take precedence.

## 2. Beta status

NIM Studio 0.1.0-beta is pre-release research software under active development. It may contain incomplete or experimental functionality, defects, changing interfaces, evolving documentation, and compatibility limitations. Features may be added, modified, suspended, or removed between beta builds.

The beta is intended to evaluate usability, data-organization workflows, reliability, documentation, and fit with research data-management needs. Participation does not guarantee access to future builds or the stable release.

## 3. Access

Executable beta packages are supplied only to approved participants through a controlled distribution channel. Access is personal to the approved participant or limited to the users expressly approved for an institutional evaluation.

Participants must not share packages, credentials, activation information, private download links, or non-public documentation with unapproved persons. NIM Studio may use build identifiers, checksums, expiry dates, or access controls to manage beta distribution and investigate unauthorized redistribution.

The public GitHub repository and Zenodo record contain documentation and citation material only. Their public availability does not grant access to, or a licence for, the application.

## 4. Permitted evaluation

Subject to the applicable beta licence, approved participants may install and use the supplied build for internal research, educational, evaluation, and research data-management activities.

Participants must use only data they are authorized to process and must review proposed operations and outputs before relying on them. NIM Studio does not replace scientific quality control, institutional governance, information-security review, or professional judgment.

## 5. Excluded uses

The beta must not be used for:

- Clinical diagnosis, treatment decisions, emergency use, or another regulated medical purpose.
- Unlawful surveillance, unauthorized access, or processing data without an appropriate legal and institutional basis.
- Production-critical workflows without independent validation, backups, and an approved recovery plan.
- Redistribution, sublicensing, resale, public hosting, or provision as a service without prior written authorization.

Detailed restrictions are contained in the beta licence.

## 6. Data protection and security

NIM Studio follows a local-first design approach. Unless a separately documented external integration is deliberately enabled, processing is intended to occur on the participant's system.

Participants remain responsible for data minimization, authorization, access control, backup, retention, deletion, incident response, GDPR compliance, ethics approvals, data-use agreements, and institutional requirements. A local-first design does not by itself establish legal or institutional compliance.

Personal data, participant identifiers, credentials, confidential datasets, or private institutional information must not be placed in public issues or ordinary feedback messages. Suspected vulnerabilities and security incidents must be reported privately through the designated contact channel.

## 7. Feedback and issue reporting

Participants are encouraged to report defects, usability problems, documentation gaps, compatibility issues, and suggestions through the designated beta channel. Reports should include the NIM Studio version, operating environment, relevant workflow stage, expected result, observed result, and sanitized logs where appropriate.

Feedback must not contain information the participant is not authorized to disclose. Treatment of feedback and any related rights is governed by the beta licence or separately signed agreement.

## 8. Scientific reporting and citation

Participants may cite the beta in scientific outputs using the version-specific metadata in `CITATION.cff` and the Zenodo documentation DOI. A citation does not constitute independent validation or endorsement of NIM Studio.

Participants should report the exact application version, execution environment, relevant workflow configuration, input-data version, and material manual interventions. Public claims about validation, performance, security, regulatory status, or institutional endorsement require an appropriate evidentiary basis and must not misrepresent the beta.

## 9. Updates, suspension, and end of beta

Beta builds may expire or be replaced for security, reliability, compatibility, or programme-management reasons. Participants may be required to stop using an obsolete build and install an updated version.

Access may be suspended or withdrawn in accordance with the beta licence or applicable agreement. On termination, participants must stop using the beta and delete supplied packages and non-public materials, except for copies that applicable law or an institutional record-retention obligation requires them to preserve.

## 10. Future releases

Future versions may use different functionality, distribution channels, access models, or licensing terms. No commitment is made to release source code, provide open access, maintain a particular feature, or grant beta participants access to future versions.

## 11. Contact

The official beta-support, security-reporting, and licensing contacts will be provided during onboarding and added to the public documentation once confirmed.

> This policy is an operational summary and is not a substitute for a reviewed beta-testing agreement or legal advice.
